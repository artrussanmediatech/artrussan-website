A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Technology with a
human perspective.

I'm Peter Albert Sansan, based in Port Moresby, Papua New Guinea. My interests sit at the intersection of ICT, web development, digital design and creative communication.

MY PERSPECTIVE

Useful work, made
with intention.

My professional work includes ICT software support, technology troubleshooting and digital projects. Alongside that work, I continue to develop my skills in website development and visual design.

I also have a creative background in Theatre Arts and Information and Communications Science. That combination influences how I think about digital experiences: technology needs to function well, but it should also communicate clearly and make sense to the people using it.

Artrussan is the space where these interests come together. It presents selected work, shares useful resources and explores the possibilities of technology and creativity in Papua New Guinea.

What guides my work

  • Clarity: make information easier to understand and use.
  • Reliability: build carefully, test thoroughly and keep improving.
  • Creativity: look for thoughtful ways to solve real problems.
